Paul Mella

Welcome to my personal webpage. I am a PhD student at Sorbonne Université (Paris). I work in the Laboratory of Probabilities, Statistics and Modeling (LPSM), under the supervision of Prof. Yves Coudène. My domains of interest are hyperbolic geometry (and more specifically non-positively curved Riemannian manifolds), dynamical systems, and ergodic theory.
